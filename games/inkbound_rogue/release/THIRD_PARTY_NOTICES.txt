Production credits

Creator, creative direction, and asset production: Joyer Huang.
Development collaboration, code, and tooling: OpenAI Codex (GPT-5).
Engine and pipeline tools: Godot Engine 4.2.2, Manga Forge, Python 3, and
FFmpeg 4.4.2. Additional visual-production and previsualization tools credited
by the project owner: ComfyUI, Stable Diffusion, and MiniMax H3.

All deterministic pixel art and synthesized sound effects in this build are
original project assets generated by committed Manga Forge/Python tooling. The
two character atlases, all seven narrative backgrounds, and ten Ink Art
cinematic images were created with OpenAI image generation under project
direction; the exact image-model version was not retained for every asset and
is therefore not claimed here. Fourteen Japanese combat-feedback performances
were generated with OpenAI Realtime `gpt-realtime-2.1`. Six Japanese Ink Art
performances were generated with a locally operated IndexTTS 2.5 pipeline. The
soundtrack uses five pre-generated AIGC tracks made with 海绵音乐 / Hai Mian
Music and supplied by the project owner. FFmpeg 4.4.2 was used for deterministic
runtime audio preparation, not composition or generative remixing.

Source hashes, known model/service identities, transformations, demo approval,
and commercial-release gates are recorded in the asset provenance files and
`release/ai-content-disclosure.md`. All AI-assisted assets are pre-generated;
the shipped game makes no live AI service requests. The precise workflow/job
records and runtime-file mapping for ComfyUI, Stable Diffusion, and MiniMax H3
must be backfilled before commercial release.
