# Cutscene art provenance

These seven project-bound backgrounds were generated with the built-in OpenAI
image generation tool for Manga Forge's `illustration-story` workflow. Runtime
dialogue remains separate from the images so future localization does not require
regenerating art.

- `prologue.png`: Nara faces living masks in the ruined Grand Archive.
- `act1-mask-memory-v2.png`: a broken mask restores the warm memory of a baker.
- `revelation.png`: Nara discovers Iori imprisoned by the Binder.
- `finale.png`: Nara and Iori confront the First Author and the blank page.
- `ending-choice-v2.png`: Nara and Iori face the two costs of the final mark.
- `ending-keep-v2.png`: the scarred Archive reopens to a living community.
- `ending-rewrite-v2.png`: the binding dissolves into an unmapped dawn.

Shared prompt constraints: hand-drawn 2D manga, layered cinematic depth, near-black
ink and warm paper with restrained crimson, landscape framing, quiet subtitle area,
no embedded words, logos, UI, speech bubbles, or watermark.

The four `v2` siblings were generated as non-destructive style- and
character-consistent transformations of the original anchors. All seven images
are 1672×941 RGB PNGs and remain unlettered; camera focus, close-up crops,
speaker accents, transitions, dialogue, and choices are authored in GDScript.
