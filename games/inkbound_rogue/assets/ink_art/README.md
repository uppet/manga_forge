# Ink Art cinematic assets

These ten runtime images are pre-generated AI-assisted content. They were
created with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool on 2026-09-02 under
project direction, reviewed as five silent MP4 prototypes, and integrated into
the game without any runtime model or API call.

## Identity and source references

Every generation used `combat-cast-atlas-v1.png` and
`nara-slash-atlas-v1.png` as Nara identity and rendering references. Cut-ins
also used the approved first Ink Art cinematic concept as a quality reference.
The shared brief required Nara's long black high ponytail, red headband and
scarf, white wrap shirt, charcoal hakama, sandals, black ink weapon, and the
project's black/ivory/crimson/gold manga-pixel palette. It prohibited text,
logos, watermarks, extra people, modern weapons, blue glow, and photorealism.

Each form received one 16:9 cut-in and one transparent five-column startup
strip:

- Marginalia / Palimpsest Ring: a completed ink circle and four cardinal cuts.
- Greatbrush / Final Period: shoulder load, overhead commitment, and a heavy
  brush-period impact.
- Needlepoint / Red Line: compressed guard, piercing displacement, and brake.
- Seal-Caster / Seal Storm: ground stamp, cardinal waves, and twelve-way
  release.
- Twin-Stroke / Cross Revision: real diagonal cut, delayed mirrored cut, and
  crossed impact.

The original generated cut-ins were 1672x941 RGB PNGs. Startup sources were
approximately 2172x724 RGBA PNGs with genuine transparency after checkerboard
extraction. Runtime derivatives are metadata-stripped 960x540 cut-ins and
1080x360 five-frame strips (216x360 per frame). LanczosSharp resizing was used;
no subject, pose, effect, color, or composition was regenerated or repainted.

The source-generation prompts and prototype timing notes are retained under
`design/previews/ink-art-cinematic-v1/` and
`design/previews/ink-art-weapon-cinematics-v1/` in the development worktree.
These assets require the same publisher rights/IP review as the other
AI-assisted illustrations before a commercial release.
