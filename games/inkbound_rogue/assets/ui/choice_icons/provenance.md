`#08070B`, `#141218`, `#EFE2C4`, `#FFF8E0`, `#D33037`, `#701820`, `#96AAB0`,
`#F2B344`. Consistent lighting, stroke weight, scale, and visual weight; serious
and dangerous, never cute, glossy, neon, comedic, smooth-vector, or
photorealistic.
