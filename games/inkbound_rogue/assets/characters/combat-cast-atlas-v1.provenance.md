OpenAI image generation under Manga Forge project direction. It entered the
repository in commit `33f8315` on 2026-09-01 and supplies the visual identity
reference for Nara, fifteen enemy/boss silhouettes, and the later four-frame

`art-bible.yaml`, and capture/regression review are the evidence currently
available.
